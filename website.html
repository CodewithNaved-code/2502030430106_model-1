Artificial Intelligence

Last edited • 2 hours ago • 14 min read

Artificial Intelligence (AI) refers to computer systems capable of performing tasks that typically require human intelligence. These tasks include learning, reasoning, problem-solving, perception, and language understanding.

AI is one of the fastest-growing technologies in the world and is transforming industries including healthcare, finance, robotics, and education.

History

The foundations of AI began in the mid-20th century with early computing pioneers exploring machine reasoning and symbolic logic. Over time, AI evolved from rule-based systems to deep learning neural networks capable of complex decision making.

Modern Applications

Modern AI systems are used in recommendation engines, autonomous vehicles, virtual assistants, medical diagnostics, cybersecurity, and large language models.

Future of AI

Experts believe AI will continue to influence nearly every aspect of society. Ethical concerns, regulation, transparency, and safety remain major discussion topics worldwide.
